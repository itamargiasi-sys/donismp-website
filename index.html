Server Rules

Please follow these guidelines to keep our community fun and safe

1. Be Respectful & Fair
Hate speech, toxicity, excessive swearing, and harassment will not be tolerated in chat or voice chat.
2. No Cheating or Exploiting
Hacks, x-ray packs, or abusing game bugs are strictly prohibited. GrimAC is active to detect unauthorized clients.
3. Respect Unclaimed Builds
Griefing or stealing near claimed areas or spawn regions is forbidden. Always claim your land!
